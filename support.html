Support — Simple税务师

For help with the Simple税务师 iOS app.

Contact

Email: support@rippaxlabs.com

About

Simple税务师 is an independent local-first study app for 税务师职业资格考试 learners. It is not official, not endorsed, and not authorized by any examination authority, certification body, government agency, or official 税务师 program.

Important Notes

  • The app is a study aid and cannot guarantee exam results.
  • The app contains original/illustrative practice content, not real exam questions.
  • The continuing education feature is for personal self-study tracking only.
  • The exam checklist is a personal planning aid and does not submit or confirm registration.
  • Progress, bookmarks, attempts, mock exam summaries, wrong answers, favorites, study notes, scheduled review items, user-entered score records, exam checklist state, and self-study entries are stored locally on the device.
  • The app can generate a local JSON export and import a user-selected JSON backup. Export files leave the device only if the user chooses to share them through the iOS share sheet; import files are read locally.

复习计划

复习计划 is a local queue for reviewing wrong answers, lessons, and notes. It is a personal study aid only, not an official notice, continuing education record, or score prediction.

成绩滚动

成绩滚动 lets users manually record exam-year scores and view a local rolling-window reminder. It is not an official score lookup, qualification record, registration confirmation, or passing guarantee.

Back Up, Export, or Restore Data

Open 更多 → 备份与导出 → 生成学习数据导出文件 to create a JSON backup of local study data, then use the iOS share sheet to save it somewhere you control.

To restore, open 更多 → 备份与导出 → 导入学习数据 JSON, choose a Simple税务师 backup, review the preview, and confirm. Importing replaces current local study data on the device and does not upload the file.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Paid study content is optional and is sold through Apple in-app purchase on iOS and Google Play Billing on Android. The 2026 Quiz Bank is a one-time non-consumable purchase. All Access monthly and yearly are auto-renewable subscriptions for current and future premium quiz-bank content while active.

Open 更多 → 题库购买与All Access on iOS, or 更多 → 购买与订阅 on Android, to view products, purchase, or use 恢复购买. Store prices, taxes, renewals, cancellations, refunds, and purchase settings are handled by Apple or Google for the device platform. Manage or cancel subscriptions through Apple ID subscription management on iOS or Google Play subscription management on Android.

Troubleshooting

If the app behaves unexpectedly, restart the device and confirm you are using the latest App Store version. To remove all local app data, use the in-app reset control or delete the app from the device.
