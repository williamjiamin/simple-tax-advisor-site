Privacy Policy — Simple税务师

Last updated: 2026-07-13

Overview

Simple税务师 is a local-first iOS education app for 税务师职业资格考试 study. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share developer-side user data. User-created data stays on the device unless the user explicitly exports and shares a local backup file. Users can also import a local JSON backup they choose; the app reads that file on device and does not upload it. Optional digital purchases are processed by Apple through App Store in-app purchase.

Independence Disclaimer

Simple税务师 is an independent study aid. It is not official, not endorsed, not authorized, and not affiliated with any government body, examination authority, certification organization, or official 税务师 program. The app makes no guarantee of passing any examination. It contains no real exam questions; all practice material is original or illustrative. The continuing education feature is a self-study tracking tool only and does not satisfy any official continuing education requirement.

No Account, No Tracking

  • No account registration or login is required.
  • No advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs are included.
  • No user data is sold, rented, or shared.

Local Storage

The current version stores study progress, bookmarks, practice attempts, mock exam summaries, wrong-answer records, favorites, study notes, scheduled review items, user-entered score records, personal exam checklist state, continuing education self-study entries, and local purchase entitlement display state locally on the device using Apple UserDefaults. This data does not leave the device.

In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

Simple税务师 offers optional in-app purchases for original digital study content: a 2026 Quiz Bank non-consumable purchase (simpletaxadvisor.quizbank.2026), All Access monthly auto-renewable subscription (simpletaxadvisor.allaccess.monthly), and All Access yearly auto-renewable subscription (simpletaxadvisor.allaccess.yearly).

Purchases, refunds, renewal status, subscription cancellation, taxes, and payment details are handled by Apple through the App Store. The app uses Apple's StoreKit framework to read entitlement status on device. The developer does not receive or store payment card details, Apple ID credentials, App Store passwords, or full purchase receipts on a developer server. The app currently has no developer backend for purchase history or account sync.

User-Initiated Export And Import

The app can create a local JSON export containing study progress, bookmarks, practice attempts, mock exam summaries, wrong-answer records, favorites, study notes, scheduled review items, score records, exam checklist state, and continuing education entries. The app can also import a user-selected JSON backup on device after preview and confirmation. The app does not upload export or import files automatically. Export files leave the device only if the user chooses a destination in the iOS share sheet.

Deleting Data

Users can delete locally stored app data with the in-app reset control or by deleting the app from their device. If a future version introduces cloud sync, accounts, analytics, advertising, crash reporting, support-ticket collection, or a developer backend, the policy and App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before release.
